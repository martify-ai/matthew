About

The man behind the movement.

Minister. Content Creator. Leadership Coach. Builder.

I'm Matthew Folawe O. — a minister of the Gospel, leadership coach, content creator, and founder based in Lagos, Nigeria. My life is built around one conviction: young people are not the future church — they are the church, right now.

I was called to ministry at a young age, and over the years, God has graced me to build movements — not just programmes. Through school outreach, orphanage fellowships, weekly discipleship gatherings, and digital missions, I carry the Gospel to where young people actually are.

Through Teen & Youth Discipleship Training (TYDT), I run a holistic discipleship and formation system for teenagers and young people — covering spiritual formation, character development, life skills, and leadership. TYDT is not just a programme. It is a movement designed to raise teenagers and youth who are prepared for God's purpose before the world defines them.

But I don't stop at the spiritual. I believe leadership development and innovation are also kingdom assignments. Through Forward Thinkers, I coach young people to think, build, and influence ahead of their time. Through Matthew AI Creators, I help people leverage technology and digital tools for impact.

I write, preach, create content, build systems, and train others. I operate in multiple spaces — and in all of them, the mission is the same: help young people become who God made them to be.

What I Stand For
  • Every young person carries potential that must be awakened, not managed.

  • The church must reach youth in the digital age — not just in buildings.

Vision & Mission

More than a programme.
A journey of transformation.

👁️
Vision

To raise a generation of spiritually grounded, purpose-driven, academically excellent, innovative, and influential young leaders who impact their generation for Christ.

🎯
Mission

To disciple, mentor, and equip teenagers and young adults through intentional training that develops character, leadership, academic excellence, innovation, and practical life skills.

🛠️ Equip participants with practical life skills
🤝 Create a community of growth, accountability, and mentorship

Program Structure

2 Years. Intentional.
Transformational.

TYDT runs on a hybrid model — 70% physical engagement, 30% virtual — combining the depth of in-person community with the reach of digital connection.

Year 1

Foundation

Building the base — salvation, identity in Christ, spiritual disciplines, character, and the fundamentals of all 5 development areas. Who am I? Whose am I? Why am I here?

Mentorship Structure

Growth beyond the classroom.

TYDT emphasises mentorship and accountability at every level. Every participant has access to guides who walk with them — not just teach them.

🎙️
Role
Facilitators
Trained individuals who lead teaching sessions, workshops, and structured learning. They bring the curriculum to life and create engaging learning environments for participants.
Teaching Workshops Curriculum delivery
🔥
Role
Mentors
Spiritually mature guides who walk alongside participants in their faith and personal development journey — modelling, discipling, and pointing to Jesus at every step.
Discipleship Spiritual guidance Modelling
👥
Role
Cohort Leaders
Leaders assigned to specific cohort groups — overseeing the progress, growth, and community health of their group throughout the program duration.
Group oversight Progress tracking Community
✅
Role
Accountability Groups
Small peer groups within each cohort where participants check in regularly, share progress, pray together, and hold one another to their growth commitments with grace and honesty.
Weekly check-ins Peer support Prayer

Participation Pathways

Two ways to be part of TYDT.

Whether you're joining personally or bringing TYDT to your community — there's a pathway for you.

👤
Personal Track

Personal Enrollment

Teenagers and young adults can apply directly to join a TYDT cohort. Participants undergo registration, onboarding, and full training through the TYDT structure.

  • Open to ages 13–30
  • Individual registration
  • Full cohort experience
  • Mentor assigned
🏛️
Institutional Track

Institutional Partnership

Churches, schools, youth ministries, campus fellowships, and Christian organisations can partner with TYDT to run the programme within their community.

  • Structured curriculum provided
  • Training & mentor support
  • Leadership dev. resources
  • Youth development strategy
