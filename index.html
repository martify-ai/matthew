About

The man behind the movement.

Minister. Content Creator. Leadership Coach. Builder.

I'm Matthew Folawe O. — a minister of the Gospel, leadership coach, content creator, and founder based in Lagos, Nigeria. My life is built around one conviction: young people are not the future church — they are the church, right now.

I was called to ministry at a young age, and over the years, God has graced me to build movements — not just programmes. Through school outreach, orphanage fellowships, weekly discipleship gatherings, and digital missions, I carry the Gospel to where young people actually are.

Through Teen Discipleship Training (TDT), I run a holistic discipleship and formation system for teenagers — covering spiritual formation, character development, life skills, and leadership. TDT is not just a programme. It is a movement designed to raise teenagers who are prepared for God's purpose before the world defines them.

But I don't stop at the spiritual. I believe leadership development and innovation are also kingdom assignments. Through Forward Thinkers, I coach young people to think, build, and influence ahead of their time. Through Matthew AI Creators, I help people leverage technology and digital tools for impact.

I write, preach, create content, build systems, and train others. I operate in multiple spaces — and in all of them, the mission is the same: help young people become who God made them to be.

What I Stand For
  • Every young person carries potential that must be awakened, not managed.
  • Teenagers are not too young for God — they are exactly the right age to be prepared.
  • The church must reach youth in the digital age — not just in buildings.

About TDT

Teenagers are not too young for God.

Purpose requires preparation. TDT creates the environment where that preparation happens — intentionally.

🔥
Spiritually Grounded
TDT builds teenagers on the Word of God, prayer, and a genuine relationship with Jesus — not just church attendance or religious routines.
🧠
Mentally Developed
We train teenagers to think — critically, creatively, and with a kingdom perspective. A renewed mind is the foundation of a transformed life.
❤️
Emotionally Healthy
Teenagers are navigating identity, peer pressure, and pain. TDT creates safe spaces for emotional growth, healing, and wholeness.
⚙️
Practically Equipped
Faith without action is incomplete. TDT trains teenagers in real-life skills — discipline, goal-setting, leadership, and service — to live out their calling.

Inspired by biblical examples:

Joseph — Prepared in the pit Daniel — Faithful under pressure David — Anointed before appointed Timothy — Young but exemplary Jesus — Grew in wisdom & stature
The 5 Core Pillars

A complete formation framework.

Every pillar is intentional. Together they build a teenager who is ready — not just for church, but for life.

01
🪞
Identity, Purpose & Vision
Helping teenagers discover who they are in Christ, why they exist, and where they are going — before the world defines them first.
02
📖
Spiritual Formation
Building a consistent prayer life, Bible study habit, and sensitivity to the Holy Spirit — turning teenagers into devoted disciples, not just church attenders.
03
🏛️
Character Formation
Integrity, discipline, humility, and excellence are not accidents — they are developed. TDT creates intentional environments for character to be built and tested.
04
🚀
Life Development & Excellence
Goal-setting, study habits, time management, financial basics, and digital literacy — equipping teenagers for real-world success as kingdom representatives.
05
👑
Leadership, Service & Impact
Every teenager is a leader somewhere. TDT trains them to lead with servant hearts — in their homes, schools, churches, and communities.
Training Structure

Four stages. One destination.

Every teenager enters at the Foundation and progresses through each stage at their own pace — with intentional support at every step.

🌱
Stage 1
Foundation Stage
Establishing the basics — salvation, identity in Christ, Word and prayer habits. Who am I? Whose am I? Why am I here? Every building starts with a foundation that can hold weight.
🔨
Stage 2
Formation Stage
Character, discipline, and spiritual depth are built here. Teenagers face real challenges — assignments, accountability, mentorship — that shape who they are becoming, not just what they know.
⚡
Stage 3
Development Stage
Skills, gifting, and leadership potential are unlocked. Teenagers discover and develop what God placed in them — through practical projects, service, and real-world application.
🚀
Stage 4
Activation Stage
Ready to be sent. Teenagers in this stage are deployed — leading others, serving communities, representing Christ in schools, and becoming the next generation of mentors in TDT itself.
Monthly Discipleship Model

Consistent. Intentional. Relational.

Transformation doesn't happen in one meeting. It happens through consistent rhythms — monthly, weekly, and daily.

📍
Monthly Physical Gathering
One focused in-person session every month — structured teaching, group activities, worship, and community building in a safe, energetic space.
👥
Weekly Mentorship Groups
Small accountability groups led by mentors — checking in on growth, reviewing assignments, praying together, and building real relationships.
📝
Assignments & Challenges
Practical tasks given between sessions — scripture memorisation, journaling, service projects, and leadership challenges that make growth tangible.
🙏
Prayer & Spiritual Rhythm
Teenagers are taught to build their own personal prayer life — not dependent on the group, but deeply connected to God individually and corporately.
💬
Digital Community (WhatsApp)
An active WhatsApp community keeps the movement alive between sessions — daily devotionals, check-ins, updates, encouragements, and shared victories.
🎯
Goal Reviews & Growth Tracking
Every teenager sets personal growth goals each month. Mentors review progress, celebrate wins, and help navigate setbacks — because growth is a journey, not an event.
