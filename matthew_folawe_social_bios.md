### About Section

I am a minister of the Gospel, a trainer, and a builder — operating across three disciplines: Discipleship, Leadership, and Innovation.

Everything I do is anchored in one conviction: the world needs people who are not just talented, but spiritually grounded, intentionally developed, and equipped to build with purpose.

---

**What I do:**

In the area of **Discipleship**, I train individuals and groups to grow in their relationship with God, discover purpose, and live as intentional disciples. My flagship initiative — TYDT (Teen & Youth Discipleship Training) — is a free, structured 2-year programme for teenagers (13–19) and young adults (20–30) covering Discipleship, Leadership, Academic Excellence, Innovation, Life Skills, and Ministerial Training. Over 3,000 young people have been reached through school outreach, church outreach, and community missions across Lagos.

In the area of **Leadership**, I coach and train emerging leaders through Forward Thinkers — a structured cohort programme designed to raise leaders who think ahead, build with purpose, and influence their generation. I believe leadership is not a position. It is a developed capacity.

In the area of **Innovation**, I train individuals in AI tools, digital strategy, content creation, and web development. Through Matthew AI Creators, I also build websites and brand identities for ministries, businesses, and personal brands — helping people show up professionally in the digital space.

---

**My background:**

I am based in Lagos, Nigeria. My work sits at the intersection of faith, leadership development, and technology — three areas I believe are not separate but deeply connected. I have been in active ministry for several years, working with teenagers and young adults across schools, churches, and communities in Lagos.

I am also a web developer and brand builder — I have built digital platforms for clients across ministry and business, combining clean design with strategic thinking.
